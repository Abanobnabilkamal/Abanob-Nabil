About

Background & Focus

Applying machine learning principles to analyze data, evaluate models, and deliver practical software.

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at Beni Suef University, focusing on applied machine learning workflows and end-to-end data pipelines.

My hands-on experience covers data cleaning, handling missing values, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualizing complex patterns. I train, compare, and benchmark predictive algorithms—such as Ridge Regression, XGBoost, and neural networks with TensorFlow and Keras—using rigorous evaluation metrics including RMSE, MAE, R², and Confusion Matrices.

To make machine learning solutions accessible and functional for end-users, I specialize in packaging models into interactive Streamlit applications and building clean, reproducible code with Git and Docker.
